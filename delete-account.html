YOUR ACCOUNT, YOUR CHOICE

Request account
deletion.

You can ask us to delete your DropOwl account and the personal data associated with it. The request is handled by email.

01 · SEND YOUR REQUEST

Tell us which account to remove.

Send an email from the address linked to your DropOwl account. Include your DropOwl username and use DropOwl account deletion as the subject. Please do not include your password or sign-in codes.

Email a deletion request ↗ info@dropowl.me
02

We verify it’s you

We may reply to confirm the request using the email address associated with your account.

03

We process the request

After verification, we’ll remove your account and associated personal data from active DropOwl systems, except where retention is required for legal, security or fraud-prevention reasons.

If you can’t access the email address on your account, explain that in your request. We may ask for other information to verify account ownership. Signing out or uninstalling the app does not submit a deletion request.
